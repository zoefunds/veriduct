## Evidence and enforcement scope

This is a production deployment of the demonstration application, not evidence of a production payments service. Both HTTP streams are deterministic synthetic fixtures generated by the same Vercel application and source tree. The two registered wallets prove distinct publisher identities and signing authority; they do not prove separate monitoring operators, probe infrastructure, hosting, or independently acquired telemetry. “Independent” below refers only to wallet ownership or validator execution where explicitly stated. Exact-byte SHA-256 matching authenticates the bytes retrieved from a frozen URL, not the real-world accuracy of values inside those bytes.

The demonstrated read-only consequence applies only to `ServiceControl.submit_operation` on the deployed GenLayer adapter. It does not control an external payments API.
